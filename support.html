To help us find a problem, say which phone you have, its system version (iOS or Android), and the app's version, and add a screenshot if you can. Please don't send passwords or anything private you typed: the keyboard keeps nothing that could help us, as it never sends anything anywhere (see the privacy policy).

Turn on the keyboard

iPhone

  1. Open Settings ▸ Kdam ▸ Keyboards and turn on Kdam Keyboard. (Or Settings ▸ General ▸ Keyboard ▸ Keyboards ▸ Add New Keyboard.)
  2. In any app, touch and hold 🌐 under the keys and choose Kdam Keyboard.

Leave “Allow Full Access” off: Kdam Keyboard doesn't need it.

Android

  1. Open the Kdam Keyboard app and tap “Turn on Kdam Keyboard”.
  2. Tap “Switch to Kdam Keyboard” and choose it.

Questions

  • Where are the settings? On iPhone, tap ⚙︎ at the left of the keyboard's suggestion bar: iOS keeps a keyboard's settings in the keyboard unless it has Full Access. On Android, open the Kdam Keyboard app, or hold the emoji key and tap the gear.
  • How do I type Khmer with Latin letters? Turn on “Type with Latin letters” (iPhone: ⚙︎ in the keyboard; Android: the app's setup screen). Then type a word as it sounds, like knhom, and pick ខ្ញុំ from the suggestions. The ក | abc toggle on the bar switches the Latin letters between romanized Khmer and English.
  • How do I switch between Khmer and English? Tap 🌐, or slide your finger sideways on the space bar.
  • Why does the iPhone show its own keyboard in some fields? iOS always uses its own keyboard for passwords and phone-number pads, whichever keyboard you chose.
  • How do I make it forget the words it learned? Use “Clear learned words” (iPhone: ⚙︎ in the keyboard; Android: the app), or delete single words in My words. On iPhone, “Pause learning” stops it learning while you type something private.
  • The keyboard suggests a wrong or missing word. Add your own words in My words, and write to us with the word: the word lists improve with each update.
